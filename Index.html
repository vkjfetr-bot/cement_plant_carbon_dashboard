Insights
Root cause · Remedial action · Timestamp
Warning—
Grid electricity is dominant source at 52%
Electricity CO₂ (41 tCO₂/day) exceeds coal CO₂ (37.1 tCO₂/day). Root cause: CEA India grid EF of 0.82 kg CO₂/kWh reflects a coal-heavy national grid. Electricity intensity is 50 kWh/ton.
Action: Evaluate on-site renewable or PPA. 10% grid substitution saves ~4.1 tCO₂/day and adds ~49 credits/month.
Critical—
Coal NCV uses IPCC default — no site-verified value
Coal CO₂ calculation uses default NCV of 20 GJ/ton. Actual coal NCV varies by supplier lot. Root cause: absence of proximate analysis. This introduces uncertainty in the 37.1 tCO₂/day figure — a CDM audit risk.
Action: Commission proximate analysis per ISO 13909 within 30 days. Assign to energy manager.
Positive—
Plant is 17% below CDM sector benchmark — credit eligible
Specific emission of 0.0789 tCO₂/ton is below the benchmark of 0.095 tCO₂/ton. All inputs are measured (not estimated). IPCC + CDM methodology applied. GHG Protocol scope classification confirmed.
Action: Initiate CDM/VCS project registration. Prepare MRV report. 5,796 credits/year eligible.
Warning—
Thermal energy intensity at 0.4 GJ/ton — room to improve
Global best practice for dry-process kilns is 0.29–0.33 GJ/ton. Root cause may be kiln age, suboptimal combustion air ratio, or process type. Coal CO₂ is 37.1 tCO₂/day (47% of total).
Action: Conduct kiln shell heat loss audit. Review combustion air-to-fuel ratio. 10% improvement saves ~3.7 tCO₂/day.
Info—
Diesel contribution is 1% — no immediate action needed
Diesel CO₂ is 0.804 tCO₂/day from 300 L/day DG consumption. EF of 2.68 kg CO₂/L (IPCC default) applied. Data from DG tank meter — measured.
Action: No remediation required. Monitor if volume exceeds 600 L/day.
